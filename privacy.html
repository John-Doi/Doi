Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026

This page explains what information DØi Labs collects through this website, why we collect it, and how it's used. It applies to www.doilabs.la.

Information You Submit

The mission intake form on this site asks for the information needed to evaluate and respond to a project inquiry, including your name, company (optional), email, phone number, project name, site address or location, service needed, and a project description. Some fields (preferred date, time window, urgency level) are optional.

This information is submitted directly by you when you fill out and submit the form. We do not collect it any other way.

How We Use It

Submitted project information is used only to:

  • Evaluate the feasibility and scope of your request
  • Respond with a quote, timeline, or follow-up questions
  • Coordinate the mission if you move forward with an engagement

When you submit the form, your information is sent to DØi Labs through a workflow automation service that routes the request to our team for review. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing to third parties.

We retain submitted project information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry and, if we proceed with an engagement, to support that project. We do not currently publish a fixed retention schedule.

Technical Information

This website does not currently use cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information generated by any web request — such as IP address, browser type, and access times — consistent with typical web server logging. This information is used only for hosting and operational purposes.

Your Choices

If you'd like to know what information we have on file from a submission, or would like it removed from our records, contact us and we'll address your request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time as the website or our processes change. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to DOiRECON@DOiLABS.LA or 310.299.7401.
