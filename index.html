MISSION MODULES

OPERATIONAL CAPABILITIES

Six integrated capabilities configure around the mission requirement — from aerial acquisition and sensing through processing, analysis, and delivery.

Systematic aerial observation and condition documentation using visual, telephoto, and thermal sensing workflows → detailed site and asset imagery for inspection support, assessment, planning, and field decision-making.
RCN
Repeatable aerial site capture for construction, infrastructure, facilities, and other operational projects → recurring progress documentation, milestone comparison, change documentation, and organized project records that support project decision-making.
OPS
Structured aerial capture and photogrammetric processing for georeferenced imagery, 3D reconstruction, spatial documentation, and project-specific mapping products, with positioning workflows configured according to mission requirements.
SYN
Thermal and visual data acquisition across applicable roofing, electrical, HVAC, solar, building-envelope, industrial, and facility applications → organized thermal findings and supporting imagery for maintenance, inspection, and planning workflows.
THM
AI-assisted processing, organization, comparison, and anomaly / area-of-interest flagging across captured aerial data — every flagged result is verified by a trained analyst before delivery → structured analytical outputs that help teams identify changes, prioritize review, and extract useful information from mission data.
XLAB
Mission requirement assessment and tailored acquisition workflow design for complex, multi-objective operations, including sensor selection, capture geometry, flight-path planning, and data-processing requirements → project-specific capture plans and deliverable configurations matched to the mission.
